Cloud computing is the on demand availability of computer resources, most commonly computation power and data storage, without active management by the user. The term is generally used to describe data centers available to many users over the Internet. Large providers run many applications on a small fraction of the total hardware, and users rent capacity instead of buying servers. Cloud services are commonly divided into infrastructure as a service, platform as a service, and software as a service. Big data workloads are natural tenants of the cloud, because clusters can be scaled up quickly for a map reduce job and released afterward. Public clouds from major vendors provide managed Hadoop and data warehouse services.
