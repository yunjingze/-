Apache Flink is an open source stream processing framework for distributed, high performance, always available, and accurate processing of unbounded and bounded data streams. The core of Flink is a distributed stream dataflow engine. It computes data streams with any size and executes programs on a cluster. Flink processes streams in event time and handles out of order records gracefully. Batch jobs are treated as a special case of stream processing. Flink saves the state of streams in fault tolerant checkpoints, so an application can resume after a failure. Companies use Flink for continuous ETL, fraud detection, and real time analytics, often as a complement to Hadoop based batch pipelines.
